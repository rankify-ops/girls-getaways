# Gallery entries removed from the portal, 15 November 2026

Tom, looking at a package page: the first two "photos" were line-drawn icons
(a high heel, a knife and fork). They are the old site's **style tiles**
("City Escapes", "Foodie Feasts", "Hens Parties"…), which sat on every package
page beside the photos and were captured into the galleries with them. 334 of
the 2,548 gallery entries were tiles, on 122 of the 123 packages. Another 50
entries were the same photo twice in one gallery.

Both were removed from `collection_items.data.gallery` in the portal for site
`0198aa57-a396-4b67-aa23-cd448025e73c`. Nothing was deleted from the media
store: the files are still there, they are just no longer in any gallery.

**What counted as a tile:** a square image whose original name starts with a
style name, with or without `Black_` / `White_` in front
(`gallery/City-Escapes-…`, `gallery/Black_Pamper-Days-…`). No hero image was
one. Two other square images (`gallery/1`, `gallery/2`) are photographs and
were left.

**What counted as a duplicate:** a later entry in the same gallery with the
same byte size, width and height as an earlier one.

The rule that keeps them out of any re-import is in the portal's repository:
`rankify-editor/scripts/style-tile.mjs`, used by `scripts/import-galleries.mjs`
and tested in `tests/gallery-style-tiles.test.ts`. By name alone it picks out
exactly the same 89 files.
